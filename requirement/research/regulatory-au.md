## NSW Supreme Court Practice Note SC Gen 23

**Source:** https://supremecourt.nsw.gov.au/content/dcj/ctsd/supreme-court/supreme-court-home/practice-procedure/generative-artificial-intelligence.html
**Full text (PDF):** https://supremecourt.nsw.gov.au/content/dam/dcj/ctsd/supreme-court/documents/Practice-and-Procedure/Practice-Notes/general/current/PN_SC_Gen_23.pdf
**Issued:** 28 January 2025 | **Commenced:** 3 February 2025 | **Amended:** (minor amendments announced, effective 3 Feb 2025)

### Key provisions:
- **Para 9B:** Gen AI MAY be used for: (a) chronologies, indexes, witness lists; (b) preparation of briefs/draft Crown Case Statements; (c) summarising/reviewing documents and transcripts; (d) written submissions (subject to paras 16-18)
- **Para 10:** Gen AI must NOT be used in generating content of affidavits, witness statements, character references or other evidentiary material
- **Para 12:** Gen AI must NOT alter, embellish, strengthen, dilute or rephrase witness evidence
- **Para 13:** Affidavits/witness statements/character references must disclose that Gen AI was NOT used
- **Para 17:** Citation verification "must not be solely carried out by using a Gen AI tool or program"
- **Para 20:** Gen AI must NOT draft expert reports without prior leave of court
- **Para 7:** Lists hallucinations, false citations, fabricated references as risks practitioners must be aware of
