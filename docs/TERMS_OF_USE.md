# Terms of use — DRAFT

> **Draft for legal review.** Publish only after review for your jurisdiction and
> business model.

## The service

LexSim AI provides simulation and preparation assistance. It does **not** provide
legal advice, does not create a lawyer–client relationship, and does not replace a
qualified lawyer.

## Your responsibilities

- You must review every AI-assisted document and verify every citation against an
  authoritative source before filing (NSW SC Gen 23 para 17 and equivalent court
  requirements).
- You must comply with the AI policy of the court/tribunal hearing the matter.
- You must not upload material you are not entitled to process, or confidential
  client communications, without appropriate authority.

## Predictions

Outcome ranges are model-generated scenario estimates, not statistical
probabilities, and are not calibrated against decided cases. Do not present them
as a guarantee or as advice.

## Prohibited documents

The service blocks affidavits, witness statements, expert reports and evidentiary
material. Do not attempt to bypass that block.

## Billing

Plans and quotas are shown in the app. Stripe handles payment; tax invoicing terms
are to be inserted before launch.

## Liability

To be completed with counsel, including consumer-law (ACL) and professional
indemnity positions.
